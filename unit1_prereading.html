Pre-reading: Why Digital Transformation Is Not a Technology Project

Key framework: WESTERMAN Digital Maturity Matrix

Digital Transformation & Artificial Intelligence · Format: Presentation

Learning Focus · ILO 1

Explain the current context of digital transformation and artificial intelligence as it relates to business.

Key framework: WESTERMAN Digital Maturity Matrix

A 2×2 classifying firms as Beginners, Fashionistas, Conservatives or Digital Masters according to how much technology they deploy and how well they govern the change.

Key Points

  • Digital intensity = investment in technology-enabled initiatives
  • Management intensity = leadership capability to drive change
  • Fashionistas buy technology without governance and underperform
  • Digital Masters outperform peers on revenue and profitability
REFERENCE

Westerman, G., Bonnet, D. and McAfee, A. (2014) Leading Digital: Turning Technology into Business Transformation. Boston: Harvard Business Review Press.
