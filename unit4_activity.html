Learning Focus · ILO 3

Define a roadmap to lead AI adoption initiatives in organisations and their business portfolios.

Adoption is not the last phase of an AI project; it is the only phase in which value is created.

The Provocation

The hardest distance in enterprise AI is the last hundred metres: from a model that works to a person who uses it.

Two minutes of silence follow. No notes. No discussion. Then three of you are asked what came to mind.

Do not explain the provocation. The discomfort of having no framework ready is the pedagogical point — it creates the demand that the gallery then satisfies.
