Course Units

Six teaching units in the EU Business School active-learning model. Each unit pairs a signature dynamic format — Presentation, Role Play, Case Study, Hackathon, Debate or Business Challenge — with a core message distilled in Kurt Bostelaar's method, its Intended Learning Outcome, a recent case, a Thumbnail-Thinking framework gallery, pre-reading, a reflection prompt, and the framework authors.
