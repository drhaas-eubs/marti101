DataHalle Rhein-Main: The Gigawatt Question

A regional coalition, a data-centre campus, and three irreconcilable accounts of the public interest

The Storyline (after Bostelaar)

Beginning · Misbelief

Each party believes the disagreement is factual and that better data will settle it. The misbelief: this is an evidence problem.

Middle · Insight

All three parties are using defensible evidence. They disagree about the boundary of the system being measured and about whose welfare counts.

End · Understanding

Impact assessment is a set of contestable choices about scope, horizon and beneficiary — and leaders must make those choices explicitly rather than inherit them.

Your Task

This case is the motion for a structured debate. You will be assigned a position — possibly one you disagree with — and expected to argue it rigorously using frameworks and evidence. You will then be required to argue the opposite side. The purpose is not to win but to discover which of your convictions survive contact with the strongest available counter-argument.
