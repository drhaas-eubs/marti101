SLIBRARY 1 — DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION FOUNDATIONS

Westerman Digital Maturity Matrix

The Digital Maturity Matrix classifies firms on two independent axes — digital intensity (investment in technology-enabled initiatives) and transformation-management intensity (the leadership capabilities that drive change) — producing four maturity types: Beginners, Conservatives, Fashionistas and Digirati.

Four maturity types from digital intensity × management intensity

REFERENCE

Westerman, G., Bonnet, D. and McAfee, A. (2014) Leading Digital: Turning Technology into Business Transformation. Boston: Harvard Business Review Press.

Rogers' Five Domains of Digital Transformation

Rogers' Five Domains framework holds that digital transformation is fundamentally a matter of strategy, not technology, and that it requires rethinking five domains of the business: customers, competition, data, innovation and value.

Digital transformation is a strategic rethink across five domains

SLIBRARY 1 — DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION FOUNDATIONS

Kotter's 8-Step Change Model

Kotter's 8-Step Change Model sets out a sequential process for leading large-scale organisational change: establish a sense of urgency, build a guiding coalition, form a strategic vision, enlist a volunteer army, enable action by removing barriers, generate short-term wins, sustain acceleration, and institute change in the culture (Kotter, 1996)..

Urgency → coalition → vision → … → anchoring

REFERENCE

Kotter, J.P. (1996) Leading Change. Boston: Harvard Business School Press.

SLIBRARY 2 — DEMYSTIFYING AI CAPABILITY

Moravec's Paradox

The observation that tasks humans find hard — abstract reasoning, calculation — are comparatively easy for computers, while tasks humans find effortless — perception, movement, dexterity — are extremely hard to automate..
